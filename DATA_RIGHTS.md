# Data rights and redistribution boundary

This repository separates **reproducibility metadata** from **rights to redistribute original images**.

## Original images

The manuscript uses material reconciled from multiple operational sources, including internal/local components and third-party repositories. The presence of an image in the analytical corpus does not by itself establish a right to redistribute that image through this repository.

Accordingly, original image files will not be released here unless their licensing and redistribution terms have been verified source by source.

## Derived research assets

Subject to final review, the repository may release derived assets such as:

- source/provenance inventories;
- image identifiers and analytical labels;
- SHA-256 and perceptual-hash metadata;
- frozen split assignments;
- exclusion/audit decisions;
- model configuration metadata;
- per-image predictions and probabilities when they do not reproduce restricted image content;
- statistical outputs, tables, figures, and reproducibility scripts.

## Historical exclusions

Historical or excluded components (including pre-generated augmentation collections, files with unresolved provenance/licensing, and deprecated experimental branches) are not part of the public-release corpus merely because they remain preserved in the private project archive.

## License

No blanket data license is asserted for third-party image content. A code license and any licenses applicable to author-generated derived data should be added only after the corresponding rights are confirmed.
