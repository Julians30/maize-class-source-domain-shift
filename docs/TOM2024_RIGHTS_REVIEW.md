# TOM2024 rights review for manuscript Figure S7

## Source identity

- Dataset: **TOM2024**
- Mendeley Data DOI: `10.17632/3d4yg89rtr.1`
- Related data article: Appiah et al. (2025), *Data in Brief* 59, 111357, DOI `10.1016/j.dib.2025.111357`
- Manuscript use: TOM2024 Category A maize images in the external evaluation and in the exploratory ResNet50 seed-17 Grad-CAM panel.

## Licence determination

The Mendeley Data record explicitly identifies TOM2024 as licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.

CC BY 4.0 permits redistribution and adaptation, provided that the reused material is appropriately attributed, the licence is identified/linked, and modifications are indicated. The Grad-CAM panel modifies the source images by adding model-activation overlays and arranging them into a new composite; therefore the final figure must identify both the TOM2024 source and the fact that the overlays/panel composition were generated in the present study.

## Release implementation

The final release-safe figure is prepared as:

- `figures/supplementary/Figure_S7_GradCAM_TOM2024_rust.pdf`
- `figures/supplementary/Figure_S7_GradCAM_TOM2024_rust.png`

The figure itself includes this attribution information so that it remains attached even when the figure is viewed outside the supplementary Word document:

> Source images: TOM2024 Category A (Appiah et al., 2025), Mendeley Data, DOI 10.17632/3d4yg89rtr.1; CC BY 4.0. Grad-CAM overlays and panel composition were generated in the present study; source images were modified by the displayed overlays.

The supplementary caption should retain the same substantive attribution and cite Appiah et al. (2025).

## Scope limitation

This rights determination is limited to the TOM2024 source material used in Figure S7. It does not establish redistribution rights for CCMT-, CIMMYT-, Kaggle-, Adege-, Pandian2019-, PlantVillage-, or any other corpus components. Those sources remain subject to independent source-by-source review before any original-image release.
